A / ARTICLES & EDITORIAL

A byline-sized disclosure.

Place a clear notice at the beginning of the publication. Extra context can live in an expandable explanation.

HTML rendering · Available in alpha
When is this appropriate?

AI-generated or manipulated public-interest text has specific disclosure duties and a qualified human-review/editorial-responsibility exception. Ordinary marketing copy is not automatically in that category. These examples are presentation choices, not legal determinations.

A correction or review must be tied to the content version; a name in a byline alone does not establish the exception.

B / IMAGES & ALTERED PHOTOGRAPHY

Let the image breathe.

A short caption can identify an artificial scene without covering it. An enlarged view must carry the disclosure too.

Image notices · Available in alpha
Generated, altered, or unknown?

“AI-generated image” describes this asset’s known origin. For an altered photograph, identify the alteration when useful: “Background altered with AI.” Do not infer that an unmarked image is human-made.

This fictional pavilion is voluntarily labeled. Whether an image constitutes a deepfake depends on its context and resemblance to real subjects; being AI-generated alone does not settle that question.

04 / THE POPUP QUESTION

One notice can explain a page.
It cannot explain away an asset.

A site-level statement can add context. A generic “everything may be AI-generated” popup does not establish that each applicable disclosure reaches people clearly and in time.

CLEAR ABOUT WHAT IT COVERS

Transparency tools.
With their own transparency.

Available in the alpha

  • One shared skill for Codex and Claude Code
  • Article and image notices in HTML output
  • Chat interaction notices before the conversation
  • Experimental media playback with captions and disclosures
  • Node and CMS/server publishing with stale-update checks
  • Portable article/image HTML exports for offline reading
  • Experimental WordPress text notices and migration tooling
  • Versioned downloads and a working Claude catalog

Before a production rollout

  • Integrate and test your actual publishing workflow
  • Resolve unknown origins and legal context
  • Verify external widgets, native clients and raw-media exports separately
  • Address provider marking duties where applicable
  • Review legal decisions and actual disclosure wording

AI Disclosure supports AI Act transparency work. It does not detect every AI asset, certify legal compliance, or replace requirements concerning privacy, copyright and misleading content. An installed skill needs a publishing integration to handle future content automatically. Independent legal review has not been obtained; this is a development release for pilot integrations.
